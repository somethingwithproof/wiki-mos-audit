Kayan aikin binciken farko na [Manual of Style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style) don muƙalolin Wikipedia.

## Dalilin samar da wannan

Ina gyara muƙalolin Wikipedia. Ba koyaushe ba, amma sosai har na fara lura da wani tsari: duk lokacin da na buɗe muƙala don gyarawa, ina bin jerin abubuwa iri ɗaya a zuciyata. Shin kwanakin wata sun dace? Akwai kalaman kauce-kauce (weasel words)? Shin wani ya bar adiresoshin URL tsirara a cikin nassoshi? Shin sashen See also ya yi yawa fiye da kima? Shin sassan ƙarshen muƙalar suna cikin jerin da ya dace?

Bayan karo na uku ko na huɗu ina duba matsaloli goma sha biyu iri ɗaya da hannu a jere, sai na rubuta wata lamba (script). Wannan lambar ta girma. Yanzu tana duba abubuwa sama da 30 da nake yi da hannu, kuma tana kama kurakuran da zan iya rasa a yammacin Talata mai gajiya.

Wannan ba ya maye gurbin hikimar edita. Kayan aikin tantancewa ne. Yana nuna maka inda za ka duba, ba abin da za ka rubuta ba.

## Abubuwan da yake dubawa

**Salo da murya**
- Kalaman kauce-kauce (MOS:WEASEL), kalaman yabo marasa tushe (MOS:PEACOCK)
- Kalaman lokaci masu dangantaka ("kwanan nan", "a halin yanzu") waɗanda ke tsufa da wuri
- Alamun rubutun da AI ta samar: yawan jingina magana a cikin jikin rubutu (WP:AISIGNS), salo irin na muƙalar ra'ayi (WP:NOTESSAY)
- Alamun kalmomin MOS:INDIGENOUS
- Tsarin sunayen MOS:AVIATION
- Bayanan asarar rayuka marasa tushe na MOS:MILITARY

**Tsari**
- Tsawon gabatarwa da adadin sakin layi (MOS:LEAD)
- Jerin sassan muƙala bisa MOS:LAYOUT
- Gajerun sassan ko waɗanda ba su cika ba
- Sassan da ba su da nassoshi (kalmomi 100+, babu `<ref>`)
- Yawan abubuwa fiye da kima a sashen See also
- Adiresoshin URL na waje tsirara a cikin jikin muƙala
- Yawan samfuran ambato (quote templates)

**Nassoshi da madogara**
- Adiresoshin URL tsirara a cikin alamomin `<ref>` (babu samfurin cite)
- Tabbatar da lambar bincike ta ISBN
- Gurɓatattun adiresoshin URL a cikin samfuran madogara
- Matattun hanyoyin haɗi na waje (na zaɓi, mai jinkiri)

**Rukuni da hanyoyin haɗi**
- Rukunin da suka ɓata
- Ingancin rukuni (ƙarancin rukuni, ko rukuni masu faɗi da yawa)
- Gano hanyoyin haɗi masu ja (wikilinks zuwa muƙalolin da babu su)
- Rukunin masu jan haɗi
- Hanyoyin haɗi zuwa shafukan warware ruɗani
- Yawan sanya hanyoyin haɗi (MOS:OVERLINK)

**Infobox**
- Adadin sigogi da cunkoson jerin abubuwa (gano kumburi)
- Sigogin da aka saba tsammani amma suka ɓata
- Alamun infobox na MOS:MILHIST

**Sauran**
- Ingancin taƙaitaccen bayani (babu shi, ya yi tsayi da yawa, ya yi daidai da take)
- Lissafin samfuran gyara
- Tsabtar hanyoyin haɗi zuwa 'yan'uwan ayyuka (MOS:SISTER)
- Gano muƙalolin marayu (babu wikilinks masu shigowa)
- Gano hanyoyin haɗi na baya masu yiwuwa
- Tsarin kanun labarai na jerin lokuta ko rahoton kai tsaye

Kowane bincike yana fitar da matsala tare da matakin tsanani (ƙasa/tsaka-tsaki/sama) da rage maki. Muƙala mai tsabta tana samun maki 100.

## Shigarwa

```
pip install .
```

mwparserfromhell abin dogaro ne da ake buƙata. Yana samar da nazarin wikitext bisa tsarin AST don gano samfura, kanun labarai, da wikilinks daidai. Idan ba za a iya haɗa shi ba (babu mai fassara C), kayan aikin yana komawa zuwa nazarin da ya dogara da regex ta atomatik.

Don haɓakawa:

```
pip install -e '.[dev]'
```

## Amfani

Bincika muƙalar Wikipedia kai tsaye:

```
wiki-mos-audit "Gettysburg Address"
```

Bincika daga URL:

```
wiki-mos-audit "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gettysburg_Address"
```

Bincika fayil ɗin wikitext na cikin na'ura:

```
wiki-mos-audit --wikitext-file article_corrected.txt
```

Bincika babban fayil na fayilolin wikitext gaba ɗaya:

```
wiki-mos-audit --dir ./articles/
```

Tsallake binciken API (yanayin rashin intanet):

```
wiki-mos-audit --wikitext-file article.txt --offline
```

### Tsarin fitar da sakamako

```
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --format text   # default
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --format json
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --json          # shorthand
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --format html
```

### Binciken zaɓi

Waɗannan a kashe suke a tsoho saboda suna da jinkiri ko suna buƙatar haɗin intanet:

```
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --check-urls       # HEAD-check all cited URLs
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --check-orphan     # check for incoming wikilinks
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --check-backlinks  # find articles that mention but don't link here
```

### Gyara ta atomatik

Za a iya aiwatar da gyare-gyaren inji masu aminci (sarari, tsara rubutu) ta atomatik:

```
wiki-mos-audit --wikitext-file article.txt --fix --output-wikitext-file article_fixed.txt
```

Duba kafin rubutawa:

```
wiki-mos-audit --wikitext-file article.txt --fix --dry-run
```

### Yanayin kwatanta bambanci (Diff)

Kwatanta wikitext na cikin na'ura da sigar da ke kan layi:

```
wiki-mos-audit --wikitext-file article_corrected.txt --diff
```

### Saiti

Ƙirƙiri fayil ɗin `.wiki-mos-audit.toml` don daidaita iyakoki ko kashe wasu takamaiman bincike:

```toml
min_severity = "low"
disabled_checks = ["mos-aviation-style"]
max_lead_words = 300
short_section_words = 40
overlink_threshold = 4
```

Bayar da shi a bayyane:

```
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --config .wiki-mos-audit.toml
```

### Daidaita harshe

Taimakon CLI, binciken matsaloli, da tsarin rahoton rubutu/HTML suna goyan bayan harsuna 45 (`en`, `de`, `es`, `fr`, `it`, `pt-BR`, `nl`, `ru`, `uk`, `ja`, `ko`, `zh-CN`, `zh-TW`, `ar`, `hi`, `pl`, `tr`, `sv`, `vi`, `id`, `af-ZA`, `am-ET`, `ar-EG`, `ar-MA`, `fr-MA`, `fr-SN`, `ha-NG`, `ig-NG`, `mg-MG`, `rw-RW`, `so-SO`, `sw-KE`, `xh-ZA`, `yo-NG`, `zu-ZA`, `bn`, `fa`, `he`, `mr`, `ta`, `te`, `th`, `ur`, `ms`, `fil`) ba tare da ƙarin wani abin dogaro ba. Zaɓin harshe yana duba `--lang` (daban da `--language`, wanda ke zaɓar bugun Wikipedia), sannan `LC_ALL`, `LC_MESSAGES`, da `LANG`, yana komawa zuwa `en` a ƙarshe. Maɓallan fitar da JSON da samfuran wikitext ba su canzawa.

```
wiki-mos-audit "Article" --lang de --wikitext-file article.txt
LANG=fr_FR.UTF-8 wiki-mos-audit --help
```

## Python API

```python
from wiki_mos_audit import audit_mos

report = audit_mos("Article Title", wikitext)
print(f"Score: {report.score}/100")
for issue in report.issues:
    print(f"[{issue.severity}] {issue.check_id}: {issue.message}")
```
